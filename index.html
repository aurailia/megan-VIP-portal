The five rules that matter this month

✦Respond, never initiate›

Your Sacral is a rechargeable battery that only switches on for work it said yes to. This is why my job is to put finished things in front of you, copy, pages, drafts, options, so your only job is the gut answer. Every one of your eleven clients came to you through response. We are building the programs the same way.

✦Wait out the wave before any yes›

Your Emotional Authority means clarity arrives over time, never in the moment. Decisions made at the top of the wave feel different at the bottom, and your body knows the difference even when your calendar does not. Big yeses get a full wave, at least a few days. There is a check in tool in this portal for exactly this.

✦Version one, never final›

Your 1st line wants everything researched before it ships. Your 3rd line learns by doing and discovering what breaks. The word finalize freezes both of them at once, for opposite reasons. So we retire that word. Everything gets to be a version, a draft, a test. The polish comes from contact with real people, which is the only research your profile fully trusts.

✦Speak when the insight is whole›

Your Ajna connects to your Throat twice, through the 43-23 channel and the 11-12 channel. That is rare, and it is the mechanical reason your whole business is explanation. It also means forced content always comes out generic, and you can feel when it does. If a piece will not come, the insight is not whole yet. That is the channel working, not you failing.

✦Rest is part of the work›

You have a defined Root pushing you to finish and a defined Will that can force things for years. Your Moon sits in the gate of aloneness and rest, the one whose deal with life is: I do the work, then I am left alone to recover. Two decades of corporate ran on the first half of that deal. Whatever plan we build together will run on both halves, with rest built in as work, not as a reward.

Your daily decision protocol

Small decisions: ask the question out loud, answer with the sound, uh-huh or uh-uh, before the mind gets a vote. If the sound is flat, the answer is not yet.

Big decisions: park it, sleep on it through at least one full emotional wave, and only commit from a calm, steady place. Excitement is not clarity. Neither is dread. Steady is clarity.
